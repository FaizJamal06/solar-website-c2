SOLAR WATER PUMPS

How to Pick the Right Pump

01
Water source — borewell or underground needs a submersible, open pond or tank takes a surface pump
02
Total head — vertical lift from source to delivery point, plus pipe friction losses
03
Daily volume — litres per day needed, not per hour, this sets the flow rate and panel size
04
Your sun hours — irradiation varies by region, a Rajasthan system will need different sizing than one in Coorg
05
Night operation — most pumps run daylight only, grid-hybrid backup available if you need 24/7 supply
